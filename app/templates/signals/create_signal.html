Signal-quality guide

What makes a signal?

Record a new or changing safety concern that requires a structured review.

Use clear evidence

State the cases, trend, literature, product-quality finding or other evidence observed.

Set the priority

Use high or critical priority where there may be serious patient risk or urgent regulatory action.

Do not include patient names or directly identifying information in the signal description.
