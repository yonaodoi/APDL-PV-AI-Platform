Type these markers in your Word template where the data should appear. Click a marker to copy it. Formatting (fonts, bold, tables, logos, headers and footers) is kept exactly as you design it.

Back to templates

How it works

  • A value: “Patient initials: {{ marker('case.patient_initials') }}”. If the case has no initials, the whole line is removed.
  • A table of several items: make a header row, then one row with markers such as {{ marker('products.product_name') }} in its cells. That row is repeated for each item; with no items the table is removed.
  • A section that may be empty or repeat: put {{ marker('#events') }} alone on a line before it and {{ marker('/events') }} alone on a line after it. Inside, use {{ marker('events.term') }} or simply {{ marker('term') }}. Put the section heading inside the markers if the heading should disappear with it.
  • Tidying is automatic: empty lines, empty table rows, and headings whose section has no data are removed; typed heading numbers (1., 2., 3.) are closed up.
  • No markers? Upload the plain form and choose “Map it for me (AI)” – you check the suggested places before anything is used.
