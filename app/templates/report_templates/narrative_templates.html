ICSR narrative templates

One approved template per case type. The narrative is built only from the structured case record, in this order: introduction, patient, suspect drugs, events and chronology, closing (actions and outcomes), causality and associated case references. A changed template is saved as a draft and must be approved by a second authorised person before it is used. Each case narrative records the template version it was made with.
